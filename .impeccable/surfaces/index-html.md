# Surface brief: home (/) — Persuade

Audience: the bride (and the groom), on a phone, arriving from Google or Instagram. Action: send an order DM on Instagram. Proof: real product photos, real prices, 1500+ orders / 120+ weekly (owner-stated). Constraints: PRODUCT.md brand commitments (ivory/gold/brown, no dark grounds, no fog overlays on photos, Aref Ruqaa / El Messiri / Tajawal, slogan prominent, فصحى copy). Existing URLs unchanged.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page opens on the visitor's own names, stretched with kashida and set on a round pearl-rimmed mirror tray, because every Salma piece carries the couple's names; it refuses the category default of a big bouquet photo under a headline with a row of equal product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ivory ground #FBF7F0, warm brown ink #3B2A1C, gold #C9A266 / deep gold #A8813F as hairlines, foil-weight numbers and the diamond divider. Silver mirror disc with a pearl ring, white calligraphy names in Aref Ruqaa, date in Western digits like the real trays. Real photos stay clear, labels sit in small ivory cards. Price list set like a boutique menu, not cards.

STORY: Visitor types two names, sees them on the tray (or the bouquet tag) with the live price, believes it is made for them and affordable, taps one button that copies the order details and opens Instagram DM.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile 390: header; H1 one line pair; the mirror disc ~300px with names + slogan inscription; groom/bride inputs side by side under it; product toggle (tray 15 / bouquet+tag 20); primary button; top of the bouquet-styles rail peeks. Desktop: H1 above, disc ~460px centred, name inputs flank the disc as place cards, button under the disc.

FORM: "Names first" — own grounded list position 2 (assigned 7 was "ring box", user chose the pick); seed key f6d6125e. Signature interaction: kashida grows into the names as they are typed, then one light sweep crosses the mirror.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
