# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
- Primary: the bride herself, choosing and ordering her own bouquet (مسكة عروس) or engagement tray, usually on her phone, after finding Salma on Instagram or Google.
- Secondary: the groom, ordering a tray/bouquet as preparation or a surprise.
- Their job: find a personalized, good-looking piece at a fair price, see real models, trust that it will arrive on time anywhere in Jordan, and order without friction.

## Product Purpose
Salma Gifts (هدايا سلمى) is a Jordan business that makes personalized occasion pieces: artificial-flower bridal bouquets, engagement/ring trays, katb-ktab packages and books, mahr boxes, graduation gifts. The website exists so that people searching Google or asking AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) about these products in Jordan find Salma, understand the offer and prices, and move to Instagram DM to order. Success = ranking for the real search terms (مسكة عروس، مسكات عروس، صينية خطوبة…) and DM orders coming from the site.

## Positioning
Every piece is prepared one by one with the couple's names and date; best quality at the best prices; pay on delivery after inspecting; delivery to all governorates. Proven volume: 1500+ orders completed, 120+ orders per week.

## Operating Context
- Ordering happens in Instagram DM (ig.me/m/salma.gifts1). Website CTA goes there; no checkout on site.
- Orders are logged and produced per delivery day; delivery price 2 JOD.
- Bouquet styles actually made (all artificial flowers, no natural flowers): توليب، كريستال، كالا، جوري، بيوني.
- Trays: mirror tray with a wooden back to prevent breaking.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML site on GitHub Pages, domain salmagifts.com, generated by `_build/build.py` (single source of truth for prices and FAQ).
- Existing URLs must never change (ranking already exists, e.g. «صينية دبل الخطوبة»).
- Site copy in Modern Standard Arabic (فصحى); search phrases people actually type (مسكة عروس، مسكة العروسة، بكج) may appear as-is in titles/headings.
- Prices: bouquet 18, hanging add-on 2, tray 15, frame package 20, basic package 22, full package 30, book 9, mahr box 18, graduation tray 13, delivery 2 (JOD).

## Brand Commitments
- Name stays «هدايا سلمى» / Salma Gifts. Never renamed to a product-specific name.
- Slogan: «لتبقى ذكرياتكم الجميلة، خالدة» — shown prominently.
- Voice: calm, classy, story-like, not pushy sales; short text, not a photo dump.
- Visual identity (project doc salma-brand-identity.md, used in reels): ivory ground, gold accent, warm dark-brown text (never black); no dark backgrounds; no white fog overlays on photos; text on photos sits in small ivory cards. Fonts: Aref Ruqaa (celebratory headings), El Messiri (body, prices), Tajawal (small text). Prices shown clearly in Western digits.
- Owner feedback on the site: no arch frames around photos, not a photo gallery, no huge hero photo, modern and middle-ground.

## Evidence on Hand
- Real product photos in `assets/img/` (bouquets incl. tulip, calla, pink; trays; packages; mahr box; graduation).
- Stats 1500+ / 120+ weekly are stated by the owner.
- No published customer reviews yet: never invent testimonials, names, ratings or aggregateRating.
- Missing photos: crystal, jouri, peony bouquet styles — need real photos from Instagram before style pages can show them.

## Product Principles
1. Be findable: each page answers one real search intent in Jordan.
2. Show, then reassure: real models first, then price, delivery, pay-on-delivery.
3. Calm over loud: fewer words, more confidence.
4. One clear action: message us on Instagram.
5. Truth only: real photos, real prices, real numbers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Arabic RTL, mobile-first (most visitors arrive from Instagram on phones), fast on slow mobile connections.
